The plan in four phases

Foundations (Oct 1 to Nov 4): argument basics, formal logic, the main Logical Reasoning question types, and how to read a Reading Comprehension passage.
Build (Nov 5 to Dec 9): remaining question types, light timing, first LawHub test, and targeted repair of weak spots.
Timed practice (Dec 10 to Jan 20): pacing, hard questions, and full tests in one sitting.
Peak and taper (Jan 21 to test day): polish weak spots, final test, LSAT Writing, then rest.

Rules that keep the hour effective

Try every question before watching an explanation. Log every miss and why. Protect Saturday (timed work) and Sunday (review). If you miss a day, just pick up the next one. Don't double up.

Dates come from LSAC and can change, so confirm on lsac.org. The registration deadline LSAC listed is December 29, 2026.
